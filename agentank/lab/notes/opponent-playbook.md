# Opponent Playbook

Use this file to track recurring opponent patterns.

## Shield / overload target

Current notes:
- Early skill usage can create double-lane bullet pressure.
- Wall-adjacent overload shots can make one bullet hit a wall while the offset bullet stays dangerous.
- Winning by stars is viable when the tank keeps map tempo and avoids direct lane trades.

Questions to test:
- Which adjacent lane should be treated as dangerous after overload fires?
- Does the danger depend on enemy direction, wall side, or map edge?
- When leading stars, should we disengage instead of firing back?

## Boost target

Current notes:
- Boost compresses star race distance.
- Close trades after freeze can still be losing if the enemy has already repositioned.

Questions to test:
- Should freeze be used earlier against boost, or saved for escape after boost?
- Which ranges are bad for direct duels?

## Shield target

Current notes:
- Freeze has low value while the enemy is shielded unless it directly wins a star or prevents death.

Questions to test:
- Should freeze be blocked during enemy shield status?
- Should the tank prioritize side movement over shooting at shielded enemies?

## Teleport target

Current notes:
- Teleport can convert a visible star into an immediate enemy capture.
- After teleport, straight-line pursuit can become a lane trap.

Questions to test:
- Should enemy star race distance be treated as zero when teleport is ready?
- Which approach vectors are safer after teleport?
